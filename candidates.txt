Gerbang (206): The gate has swollen in the rain. A gardener sets a thumb beneath the latch and lifts it just enough. The gate swings inward. Beyond it, wet leaves shine along the path. He carries the empty basket through.
Penjilid buku (228): The bookbinder lays a strip of linen inside the torn cover. Paste shines beneath her thumb. The book rests under a smooth stone until morning. Then she lifts the cover. The linen bends, and the first page lies open on the table.
Tambalan sweter (236): A woman slips a wooden egg into the worn elbow of a sweater. Her needle goes over, under, over. Blue thread gathers across the thin place. Next morning, she pulls it on and reaches up to open the window. The blue stitches move with her.
Siput (237): A snail reaches the seam between two paving stones. Its feelers pass over first, then the slow fold of its body. The shell tilts and slowly rights itself. In the slanting light, one silver line runs over the seam and on toward the grass.
